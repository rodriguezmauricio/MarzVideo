# AI spike: whisper.cpp and Claude Code (M2 Task 1)

Date: 2026-11-19. Laptop: RTX 3060 Laptop GPU (6 GB), whisper.cpp v1.9.5 (`b5454` CUDA 12.4 build and the CPU build), models `ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0.bin` (574 MB), `ggml-small.bin` (488 MB), `ggml-silero-v5.1.2.bin` (VAD, 0.9 MB). Claude Code 2.1.327, model `sonnet` (Sonnet 5.5).

Takes used: Mauricio's existing `2026-11-17 19.35` (8 min, a Portuguese call) and `2026-11-17 19.14` (17 min, Portuguese). His three planned test takes (pt-BR, English, switching, with deliberate ums and mistakes) are not recorded yet: Step 1 and the ums-survival count stay open (list at the end).

Throwaway scripts lived in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MarzSpike\scripts` (not in the repo).

## Whisper

| Variant | 8 min take | 17 min take | Per audio minute |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPU turbo, whole file, no VAD | 25.8 s | 49.8 s | 2.9 to 3.2 s |
| GPU turbo, whole file, VAD | 15.4 s | 26.5 s | 1.6 to 1.9 s |
| GPU turbo, per section (the app: FFmpeg copy, 30 to 120 s sections, one run each, VAD) | 34 s | | about 4.3 s |
| CPU small, first 2 min, 8 threads | 33.3 s (2 min) | | 16.6 s |

1. **VAD is required.** Without it Whisper wrote words over silence: on the 17 min take 114 words sat on a silent mic (a 25 s quiet stretch became "Só que... Uhum. Tu... Ã... In... Ah,"). With VAD: 7. Word times without VAD drifted after pauses: the 8 min take's first word was placed at 0.00 s while he starts speaking at 4.0 s; with VAD it lands at 4.14 s. Share of words with voice under them: 89.8% without, 98.9% with (2 min sample). VAD also found more words (371 against 293 in the same 2 minutes).
2. **`-ot/-d` with `--vad` ignores the window** (a 45 s window returned 60 s of words), so sections are separate WAV files written by the app.
3. **DTW token timestamps** need `-nfa` (flash attention off) and gave no visible gain; not used.
4. **Filler prompt:** natural speech kept 15 to 18 "um" per 8 minutes with or without the prompt (18 without, 15 with); the prompt also dropped a few words (935 against 882). Kept for now; his deliberate-um takes decide (stop rule below).
5. **Language per section:** both takes are Portuguese and every real section came back `pt`. One near silent section (only "Mm-hmm.") came back `en`, so the languages shown in the inspector now count only sections with 5 or more words. The switching take is still needed to check real switching.
6. **CPU fallback** (`small` on CPU) is about 5 times slower than the GPU and is not wired as an automatic fallback yet: the CUDA build is the one installed.

## Claude Code (`claude -p`)

Command as in the plan, plus `--effort high`: `claude -p --output-format json --json-schema <schema> --tools "" --no-session-persistence --strict-mcp-config --setting-sources "" --model sonnet --effort high`, prompt on stdin, started in an empty temp folder, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` removed from its environment so it always uses the subscription.

| Run (8 min take, 913 words) | Wall time | Output tokens | Reached the end of the take |
|---|---|---|---|
| default effort | 3.5 s | about 280 | no, stopped at about 2:45 of 8:00 |
| `--effort high` | 13.5 s | about 1,800 | yes (last pick at 7:44) |
| in the app (high) | about 15 s | | yes |

1. Input is about 13,400 tokens for 8 minutes of speech. The JSON reports an equivalent of 0.05 to 0.07 USD per call: it counts against his plan's limits, nothing is billed.
2. Picks were right on the obvious ones: "Cara, isso é muito maneiro, isso é muito maneiro" (repeated take), "Mas então, assim, o que eu ia falar é, eu tô falando assim" (restart), "tipo assim, é uma forma muito fácil de fazer, é. É uma forma muito fácil de fazer" (repeated take), "Não, não" (repeated word). Context fillers: "tipo", "né", "assim", "sabe", "então" where they were filler.
3. Weak spots: default effort once cut a meaningful sentence ("Porque antes era complicado pra caralho", a repeated idea, not a repeated take); runs differ (3 or 5 mistakes on the same take). High effort is the default; the validator refuses any answer that would cut more than half the words.
4. The real "not signed in" and "usage limit" messages were not captured (he is signed in and under the limit); the parser matches the known CLI texts ("Please run /login", "usage limit reached") and is unit tested on those.

## Settings chosen (Global Constraints)

Silence gap 800 ms, pace 300 ms (slider 80 to 600 ms), VAD on, sections 30 to 120 s split at pauses of 1 s or more, GPU turbo by default, Claude `sonnet` with high effort, caption lines of 32 characters, highlight lines of 1 to 4 words, AI chunks of 20 minutes with 30 s overlap above 30 minutes. Unchanged from the plan except VAD and the effort flag.

## Stop rule

The plan's stop rule (fewer than half of the deliberate ums survive in every variant) cannot be judged without his deliberate takes. In natural speech Whisper kept "um" 15 to 18 times per 8 minutes, so filler cuts by transcript look viable.
